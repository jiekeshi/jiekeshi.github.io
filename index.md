<h2 style="margin: 70px 0px 10px;" id="about-me"></h2>

I am Jieke Shi (史杰克), a Research Scientist and NRF Postdoctoral Award Recipient at the [School of Computing and Information Systems (SCIS)](https://computing.smu.edu.sg), [Singapore Management University (SMU)](https://www.smu.edu.sg), where I lead the S$250,000 NRF-funded project *Optimizing and Fortifying AI-Assisted Software Ad Absurdum*. I am working with Vice Provost (Research) and OUB Chair Professor [David Lo](http://www.mysmu.edu/faculty/davidlo/), an ACM/IEEE/ASE Fellow, who also supervised my PhD at SMU.

My research spans software engineering, program synthesis, performance engineering, software testing, and large language models (LLMs). I design and develop **synthesis-assisted techniques** for (1) **performance optimization of AI systems**, such as compressing, greening, and compiling code models to make them more deployable and sustainable; and (2) **safety validation of software systems**, such as compilers, AI-enabled control systems, and trusted software. I have published [43 papers](/publications/) in top-tier SE and security venues (e.g., ICSE, FSE, ASE, TSE, TOSEM, IEEE S&P), including 16 as first/co-first/corresponding author. Beyond academic publications, my research has also had real-world open-source impact: I have reported **504 bugs, vulnerabilities, and license violations** in widely-used projects such as **GCC, Clang, OpenSSL, curl, FFmpeg, and OP-TEE**, of which **246 have been confirmed or fixed**. My work has been recognized with multiple academic honors, including **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards ([ASE'22 nomination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07120), [ASE'25 winner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12285), [ICSE'26 winner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14838))**, an **IEEE Computer Society TCSE Distinguished Paper Award ([SANER'26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17363))**, an **IEEE Computer Society Best Paper Award ([TSE'24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02496))**, a **SAC Highlight Award ([ACL'26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01002))**, an **Honorable Mention Award ([ACSAC'22](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/sis_research/7682/))**, a **Distinguished Reviewer Award (AIware'26)**, a **Distinguished PC Member Award (ICSME'26)**, and research grants from OpenAI and Google Cloud. Within SMU, I was honored with the **SMU Presidential Doctoral Fellowship (2023)**, **SCIS Research Excellence Award (2025)**, **SCIS Dean’s List Award (2023&2025)**, and **SMU Research Staff Excellence Awards (2025)**.

Before coming to SMU, I received a B.Eng. degree in Electronic Engineering from Yangzhou University (advised by Professor Junwu Zhu). I also worked as a research assistant at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (advised by Dr. [Shi Wang](https://ictkc.github.io/)).
