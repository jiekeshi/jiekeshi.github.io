<h2 style="margin: 70px 0px 10px;" id="about-me"></h2>

I am Jieke Shi (史杰克), a Research Scientist and NRF Postdoctoral Award Recipient at the [School of Computing and Information Systems (SCIS)](https://computing.smu.edu.sg), [Singapore Management University (SMU)](https://www.smu.edu.sg), where I also obtained my PhD. I am working with Professor [David Lo](http://www.mysmu.edu/faculty/davidlo/), an ACM/IEEE/ASE Fellow, as my supervisor.

My research lies at the intersection of Software Engineering (SE) and Artificial Intelligence (AI), with a particular focus on program synthesis techniques for enhancing the efficiency and reliability of AI-powered software. Over the past four years, my work has centered around two key challenges: (1) **compressing and “greening” code models to make them more deployable and sustainable**; and (2) **ensuring the safety of AI-enabled systems across both runtime and offline phases**. This research trajectory has led to [37 publications](/publications/) in top-tier SE and security venues (e.g., ICSE, ASE, TSE, TOSEM, IEEE S&P), including 13 as first/co-first/corresponding author. Beyond academic publications, my work has also delivered tangible impact on the open-source community — helping detect and fix **360+ bugs, vulnerabilities, and compiler defects** in widely-used projects such as **rustc, GCC, OpenSSL, curl, FFmpeg, and the OP-TEE ecosystem**. My work has been recognized with multiple academic honors, including **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards ([ASE'22 nomination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07120), [ASE'25 winner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12285), [ICSE'26 winner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14838))**, an **IEEE Computer Society TCSE Distinguished Paper Award ([SANER'26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17363))**, an **IEEE Computer Society Best Paper Award ([TSE'24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02496))**, an **Honorable Mention Award ([ACSAC'22](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/sis_research/7682/))**, and research grants from OpenAI and Google Cloud. Within SMU, I was honored with the **SMU Presidential Doctoral Fellowship (2023)**, **SCIS Research Excellence Award (2025)**, **SCIS Dean’s List Award (2023&2025)**, and **SMU Research Staff Excellence Awards (2025)**.

Before coming to SMU, I received a B.S. degree from Yangzhou University (advised by Professor Junwu Zhu). I also worked as a research assistant at the Institute of Computing Technology, China Academy of Sciences (advised by Dr. [Shi Wang](https://ictkc.github.io/)).
